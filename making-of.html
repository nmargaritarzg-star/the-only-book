Making of

The idea

A world with only one book: if you read it, you know everything. The story asks what we lose when every answer is ready-made — a question that felt very close to a week about AI and education. The Book is not evil and it is never wrong; the danger is that answers close questions. The ending keeps the knowledge (Kai still finds the water) but gives back the wondering.

How AI was used

Text assistant (Claude): brainstorming the premise, characters and locations; testing alternative plots and endings; drafting the three parts; editing for consistency (999 steps, eleven pockets, silver eyes, the question “What colour is music?” as a motif).

Image generator (Midjourney v7): eight images from the prompts below. The first portrait of Mira became the Style Reference for every other image, so the site looks like one book. Problem: with a strong style reference Kai and Orren came out as copies of Mira. Fix: describe opposite features first (black curly hair, tanned skin, bald head, white beard) and block Mira's features with --no copper hair, girl. The first tower had shelves full of books, which contradicted the story, so it was regenerated with --no books.

Plot alternatives we rejected

Mira steals the Book and hides it (too simple — the city would just suffer); Kai finishes reading and Mira replaces him as Keeper (too dark); the Book is burned (loses the point that knowledge is valuable). The chosen ending: the Book stays, but it is no longer the only one.
